# ✅ ServiceNow CIS-RC (Risk & Compliance)

Passed: July 2026

## The Goal
Master the compliance logic.

## What this covers
- Bringing in frameworks like ISO 27001 and NIST as **authoritative sources**, then breaking them into **citations**
- Linking citations to **control objectives** and **policy statements**, which become the controls each part of the business is tested on
- Writing **risk statements**, assessing risks and tracking them in the risk register
- Turning failed controls into **issues** with owners and due dates
- Policies, controls, and risk registers in automated GRC / IRM workflows
- Hands-on: builds 01 to 03 (risk register, policy mapping, automated evidence) in [PDI-Labs-and-Builds](../PDI-Labs-and-Builds)
